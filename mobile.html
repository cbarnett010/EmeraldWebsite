Common Questions

Mobile & Hospital Notary FAQs

Can a notary come to a hospital room?

Yes. We provide bedside notary visits for patients in hospitals, nursing homes and care facilities, traveling to you within a 50-mile radius of our office in Emporium, PA. Please allow 24 hours for scheduling when possible so we can fit the visit into our schedule.

How far will you travel for a mobile notary appointment?

We travel within a 50-mile radius of our office at 116 South Cherry Street, Emporium, PA 15834. Travel fees are based on distance and start at $20 for the first 15 miles on weekdays. For distances greater than 45 miles, contact us for a custom quote.

How much notice do you need for a mobile notary visit?

Please allow 24 hours where possible. Submit a request through the form on this page or email MobileNotary@EmeraldNotary.com with your name, address, preferred date and document details. We will confirm the appointment, discuss the documents to be notarized, and provide a travel fee estimate based on your location.

What does a mobile notary visit cost?

You pay the standard Pennsylvania notarial act fee plus a travel fee based on distance. Weekday travel fees are $20 for 0 to 15 miles, $40 for 15 to 30 miles and $60 for 30 to 45 miles; after-hours and weekend rates are higher. The full table is on our Fees page.

If the signer cannot meet in person at all, our Remote Online Notary service notarizes documents by live video, 24/7.
